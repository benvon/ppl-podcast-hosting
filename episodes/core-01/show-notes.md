## Production notice

This podcast uses AI-assisted production. The voices in this episode are AI-generated, not human speakers. Each episode's factual content is reviewed against cited source material before audio production, but it is not reviewed by a certificated flight instructor and is not flight instruction. Always use current FAA information, applicable regulations, and your aircraft's approved documents.

## In this episode

- ADM as a continuous decision process rather than a one-time go/no-go exercise.
- The difference among a hazard, its risk, and a risk control.
- PAVE, 3P, CARE, and TEAM as FAA guidance models for structured thinking.
- Personal minimums as individual planning guardrails—not regulations and not aircraft limitations.
- Hazardous attitudes, bias, workload, and external pressure.
- A fictional VFR cross-country scenario that practices identifying alternatives early.
