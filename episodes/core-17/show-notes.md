# Charts, Pilotage, and Dead Reckoning

**Episode:** 17
**Version:** 0.1.2

## In this episode

A completely fictional daytime VFR cross-country turns a sectional into a usable navigation picture. The learner first identifies terrain, obstructions, airspace, and route features; then chooses prominent pilotage checkpoints, converts a true course into a wind-corrected compass heading, builds a simple navigation log, and uses actual checkpoint position and elapsed time to revise groundspeed and ETA without guessing at the cause of a mismatch.
