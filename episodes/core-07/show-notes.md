# Flight Instruments and Failure Recognition

**Episode:** 7

**Version:** 0.1.4  
**Source verification:** FAA links, cited pages, claim relevance, and show-note mappings reviewed 2026-08-24.

## In this episode

- Trace the pressure sources behind the airspeed indicator, altimeter, and vertical-speed indicator.
- Recognize why a blocked pitot source and a blocked static source create different instrument stories.
- Connect gyro behavior and power sources to useful cross-checks.
- Separate magnetic variation, aircraft-specific deviation, dip-related compass errors, and oscillation.
- Practice reading an abnormal indication by its source and failure path rather than following one gauge alone.
