**Episode:** core-02
**Version:** 0.2.0 — approved script draft

Each episode's factual content is reviewed against cited source material before audio production, but it is not reviewed by a certificated flight instructor and is not flight instruction. Always use current FAA information, applicable regulations, and your aircraft's approved documents.

## In this episode

- Relative wind, chord line, and angle of attack.
- Why pitch attitude is not the same thing as angle of attack.
- Lift as a complete airflow and pressure picture, not a one-slogan explanation.
- Protecting useful airflow over the airfoil by understanding angle of attack and the changing flight condition.
- Parasite drag, induced drag, and the basic total-drag tradeoff.
- The relationships that later support slow-flight, stall, and performance study.
