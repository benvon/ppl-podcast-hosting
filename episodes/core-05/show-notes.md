## In this episode

- Follow the power chain from controlled combustion to piston motion, crankshaft rotation, propeller thrust, and the indications a pilot sees.
- Slow the four-stroke cycle down into intake, compression, power, and exhaust, then see how multiple cylinders keep the crankshaft turning.
- Treat the propeller as a rotating airfoil and explain blade twist through the changing speed from hub to tip.
- Compare fixed-pitch and constant-speed propellers without treating rpm as a universal measure of power.
- Separate normal combustion, detonation, and preignition by when and how cylinder pressure develops.
- Explain dual magnetos, the purpose of the ignition check, and why a propeller must always be treated as capable of firing the engine.
