# Cross-Country Planning, Fuel, and Diversions

**Episode:** 19
**Version:** 0.3.1

## In this episode

In the days and hours before a fictional daytime VFR flight from Meadow Ridge to Harbor Field, current route, weather, airport, and airplane information supports a go decision with margin and a prepared Pine Valley option. The pilot manually completes a route-planning navigation log from verified inputs, uses it to calculate trip fuel and the legal predeparture fuel load, sets a separate personal landing-fuel floor, compares actual checkpoint progress, fuel use, and weather with the plan, and diverts when Harbor crosses the weather trigger while Pine Valley remains suitable.
