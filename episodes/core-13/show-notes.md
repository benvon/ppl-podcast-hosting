# Weather Information and Preflight Decisions

**Episode:** core-13

**Version:** 0.1.1

**Source verification:** Version 0.1.1 source-relevance review is complete.

## In this episode

Using an explicitly hypothetical VFR trip from Northern California to Wisconsin, this episode connects METARs, SPECIs, PIREPs, surface analysis, GFA layers, TAFs, winds aloft, advisories, and briefing types to the decisions they are meant to support. The lesson organizes weather evidence by route segment and time, shows how to identify each product’s limits, and builds clear triggers for delay, reroute, diversion, or landing short.
