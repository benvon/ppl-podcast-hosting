# Weight, Balance, and Center of Gravity

**Episode:** core-09
**Version:** 0.1.4
**Source verification:** Publication-day FAA/eCFR link, locator, show-note, and claim-relevance validation completed August 26, 2026.

## In this episode

- Why total weight and center of gravity are separate limit checks.
- How datum, arm, and moment turn a physical loading into a calculation.
- Which current aircraft-specific data must anchor a valid loading decision.
- How computational, graph, table, and electronic methods express the same relationships.
- How forward and aft CG affect stability, control forces, and control authority.
- How loading weight feeds takeoff, climb, cruise, and landing performance planning.
- Why fuel burn requires weight-and-CG checks across relevant phases of flight.
- How to correct an unacceptable load without creating a new fuel or performance problem.
- Why every item must remain secured at the station used in the calculation.
