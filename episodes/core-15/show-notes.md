# Traffic Patterns, Nontowered Operations, and Right of Way

**Episode:** 15
**Version:** 0.1.11

## In this episode

This lesson follows a training flight to fictional Pine Valley Municipal. It connects a current airport picture, CTAF communication, visual scanning, pattern entry, spacing, and right-of-way decisions without treating a radio report or a published plan as a clearance.
