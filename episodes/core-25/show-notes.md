# The Checkride Oral: Connecting Knowledge, Risk, and Skill

**Episode:** 25 (Core)
**Version:** 0.1.3

## In this episode

- Follow Elena and Jonah's proposed daytime trip from Meadow Ridge to Harbor, with Pine Valley prepared before departure.
- Rehearse short examiner-style questions, reasoned applicant answers, and follow-ups that change one assumption at a time.
- Connect pilot readiness, airworthiness, weather, loading, performance, fuel, cockpit preparation, diversion, systems, approach, and postflight.
- Understand why practical-test questioning continues in flight and why this sample conversation must be paired with preparation across all applicable ACS tasks.

## Study materials and practice

Use the deep links below with your instructor and the current information for your actual airplane. Bring a proposed flight, show where each input came from, and practice explaining what changes when an input changes. The airport names and arithmetic are fictional teaching examples, with no model-specific performance numbers.

Suggested practice: change the departure time, loading, wind, destination weather, or available equipment one item at a time. Identify the new evidence needed, recalculate what changes, then choose the next action. Keep flight control, position, terrain, traffic, and weather in view while using cockpit resources.
