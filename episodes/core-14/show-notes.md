# Airport Operations and Surface Safety

**Episode:** core-14
**Version:** 0.1.12

## In this episode

- Build an airport-surface picture from the current diagram, NOTAMs, ATIS or other current airport information, and the airplane's known position.
- Translate a taxi clearance into route segments, runway crossings, and hold-short limits before moving.
- Use location, direction, and mandatory signs as answers to three questions: where am I, where does the route go, and what protected boundary is ahead?
- Recognize runway holding-position markings, explicit crossing clearances, runway-status-light conflicts, and the whole-aircraft test for clearing a runway.
- At night, use published airport-lighting information, pilot-controlled lighting where installed, and runway-versus-taxiway light colors without letting any single cue replace the full position check.

The daytime and nighttime airports, geometry, routes, runway identifiers, taxiway letters, clearances, and lighting activation described in the episode are hypothetical teaching frames. Use current airport publications and instructions for an actual operation.

- [Section 2-3-8 of the AIM: mandatory instruction signs](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap2_section_3.html#2-3-8) — runway entrances, protected areas, and no-entry signs.
- [Section 2-3-9 of the AIM: location signs](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap2_section_3.html#2-3-9) — taxiway location and runway-boundary visual aids.
- [Section 2-3-10 of the AIM: direction signs](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap2_section_3.html#2-3-10) — taxiway designations and turn arrows.

- [Section 2-1-8 of the AIM: pilot-controlled lighting](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap2_section_1.html#2-1-8) — system variations, activation timing, and seven/five/three-click table.
- [Section 2-1-9 of the AIM: airport and heliport beacons](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap2_section_1.html#2-1-9) — beacon color combinations and weather-interpretation limitation.
- [Section 2-1-10 of the AIM: taxiway lights](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap2_section_1.html#2-1-10) — blue edge lights and green centerline lights.
- [Section 4-3-24 of the AIM: use of aircraft lights](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap4_section_3.html#4-3-24) — anti-collision, rotating-beacon, taxi-light, runway-crossing, and strobe-light guidance.
