# VFR Operating Rules and Preflight Responsibilities

**Episode:** 22
**Version:** 0.1.9

## In this episode

Elena and Jonah prepare a daytime VFR trip. A blank installed display leads Elena to change airplanes and recheck loading for two people, bags, and fuel. A destination runway closure leads her to choose another landing airport and recalculate fuel. En route, slower groundspeed leads her to use a prepared fuel stop, cancel the gathering, and fly home before sunset. The lesson connects pilot-in-command responsibility, current preflight information, an appropriate passenger briefing, and altitude choices to those decisions. Episode 9, *Weight, Balance, and Center of Gravity*, teaches the loading calculation. Episode 8, *Aircraft Documents, Airworthiness, Inspections, and Maintenance*, teaches the detailed aircraft equipment method. Episode 21, *Private-Pilot Privileges, Limitations, and Currency*, explains Elena's passenger-currency choice.
